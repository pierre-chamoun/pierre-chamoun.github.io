abstract: >
  Transferring manipulation policies from simulation to the real world remains challenging due to
  the difficulty of modelling contact dynamics. We introduce an adaptive domain randomisation
  schedule that gradually increases the diversity of simulated environments based on a measure of
  policy robustness. On a suite of contact-rich tasks — peg insertion, gear assembly, and cable
  routing — our approach reduces the simulation-to-real performance gap by 41% relative to
  fixed-schedule baselines.
